Our Promises

  • Always Opt-In
    • Data collection will always be opt-in, transparent, and plain-spoken.
    • We collect data strictly for statistics and will never sell your information.
    • All details are fully stated in our Privacy Policy.
  • No Planned Obsolescence
    • We will never intentionally slow down, restrict, or design our software to become obsolete to force an upgrade.
    • We only retire older apps or versions when maintaining security and compatibility is no longer practical.
  • Where Form Meets Function
    • Our software is built for accessibility and versatility—simple enough for immediate home use with zero setup, yet powerful enough for professional environments.
*These promises only apply to our own products, not commissioned software
